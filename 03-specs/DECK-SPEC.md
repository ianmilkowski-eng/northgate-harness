## Visual system (owner-approved; follow exactly)

- **Material:** full liquid glass (Apple iOS 26). It must read unmistakably as glass: visible translucency with the field flowing through, a lensing band where the grid bends at the edges, a crisp specular rim (bright top edge fading down the sides, faint darker lower rim for thickness), a soft broad sheen across the top of the slab, and a large, soft, blue-tinted shadow (not grey). Current kit reads as frosted white cards; push it: lower white tint (~30–40%), stronger lens band, visible sheen. Keep technique (d): bake the material into the plate, keep text/charts live and vector.
- **Field:** pale blue studio `#EEF3F9` with a hint of cream `#F7F4EE` (warmth in one corner), light-blue `#CFE3F7` and lavender `#E4DCF8` washes used as long, soft light bands that pass BEHIND the glass (so the glass has colour to refract). No orbs, no blobs. Keep the hairline grid but calmer: larger cells, lower contrast in open areas.
- **Ink:** `#18202E`; secondary `#5B6475`; muted `#8A93A3`; hairlines `#D9E2EE`. Accent lavender `#B9A8F0`. Data blue `#5A86D8` (deep variant `#3F6FCB` for the one emphasised mark); data violet `#9A86E0` only for reference rules/annotations, never as a second series next to blue (CVD).
- **Type:** Inter Tight (display: titles, hero numbers; 500–600, tracking −0.02 to −0.045em) + Inter (everything else, 400–500). Tabular figures in all tables/charts; proportional figures in hero numbers. Until Inter arrives the fallback is Instrument Sans; leave ~8% horizontal slack in every line. Sentence case everywhere, no all-caps labels.
- **Scale (px @1920):** eyebrow 22/500 secondary; title 60–64 Inter Tight 600; hero 200–230 Inter Tight 600; hero line 38–40 Inter 400 secondary; body 24–26; labels 20; source 18 muted.
- **Grid:** margins 120 left/right, 96 top, 84 bottom; 12 columns, 24px gutter. Eyebrow top-left at the same position on every slide. Source line bottom-left, glass page pill bottom-right ("03 / 15"), same position on every slide.
- **Glass usage:** one main slab per slide (the proof: chart or table) + the page pill. Cover and slide 2 may use one larger hero slab. Never a grid of glass cards.
- **Charts:** plain SVG, drawn to scale; bars 22–28px thick with 4px rounded data-ends anchored to the baseline; 2px gap between adjacent fills; direct labels, no legend when one series; recessive or no axes; label text in ink tokens, never series colour.
- **Basis tags:** tiny outline capsules (1px hairline, 16px Inter 500 secondary): Measured / Contractual / Modeled.
- **Banned:** gradient text, emoji, icons in circles, glows/neon, drop-shadow on text, bento grids of cards, decorative numbering that isn't a real sequence, all-caps, exclamation marks.
